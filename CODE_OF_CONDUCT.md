# Community conduct

Communicate respectfully, assume good faith and focus reviews on observable
behavior and reproducible evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sharing
private information and deliberate disruption are not welcome.

Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation when needed to protect
contributors. Explain concerns privately to repository maintainers when possible;
do not publish another person's private information in a report. Vulnerabilities
should follow SECURITY.md rather than public discussions.
